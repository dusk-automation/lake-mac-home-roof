Questions

Before you ask.

Is any job really too small?

No. We started this business for the jobs the bigger companies don’t want. If it’s on your list, send a message and ask.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Lake Mac Home & Roof Co. is fully licensed, qualified and insured. Licence number to add with the owner.

Which areas do you cover?

Lake Macquarie and surrounds. Recent jobs include Booragul, Charlestown, Belmont, Warners Bay, Speers Point and Toronto.

Can I send photos instead of booking a visit?

Yes, please do. Photos help us understand the job before we come out, and sometimes they’re enough to give you an idea of the price.

Do you fit gutter guard on tile roofs?

Yes. Several of the jobs on our work page show mesh gutter guard fitted along terracotta tile roofs.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text 0468 390 771, email lakemachomeroofco@gmail.com, or fill in the quote form on the contact page.
