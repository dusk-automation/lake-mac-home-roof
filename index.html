What we do

Roofs, gutters and everything in between.

Roofing and guttering is our speciality. Around it sits the carpentry and maintenance list most homes have, and we’re just as happy with the small jobs as the big ones.

  1. 01

    Roofing

    Leaks found and fixed, rusted screws and sheets replaced, new flashing, and metal re-roofs for patios and houses.

    More on roofing
  2. 02

    Gutters & gutter guard

    New gutters, fascia covers and downpipes, blocked gutters cleared, and mesh gutter guard to help keep the leaves out.

    More on gutters
  3. 03

    Patios & verandahs

    Patio roofs re-sheeted or rebuilt, verandah ceilings lined, and posts, rails and balustrades put right.

    More on patios
  4. 04

    Carpentry & renovations

    Stairs, floors, wall panelling, doors and the built-in entry you’ve been picturing.

    More on carpentry
  5. 05

    Windows, doors & cladding

    Old windows swapped for new and flashed in, and tired weatherboards and gable cladding replaced.

    More on windows, doors
  6. 06

    Tiling & repairs

    Small tiling jobs like a sandstone mosaic laundry, floorboard patches and the general repairs list.
