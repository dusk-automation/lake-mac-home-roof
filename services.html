01 / Roofing

Roof repairs and re-roofing

Jack has worked as a metal roofer for five years. Whether it’s one leak, a run of rusted screws or a whole new roof on a patio or house, the work is done carefully and finished properly.

  • Leaks found and repaired
  • Rusted screws and sheets replaced
  • New flashing and cappings
  • Metal re-roofs
  • Patio roofs re-sheeted
  • Small roof fixes

02 / Gutters

Gutters, fascia and gutter guard

Gutters that overflow, sag or leak cause damage you often don’t see until later. We replace gutters and downpipes, fit fascia covers, and install mesh gutter guard to help keep leaves out.

  • New gutters and downpipes
  • Fascia covers
  • Gutter guard on tile and metal roofs
  • Blocked gutters cleared
  • Leaking joints and corners fixed
  • Gutters re-set so water reaches the outlet
Ask about gutters

Questions

Good to know.

Is any job really too small?

No. We started this business for the jobs the bigger companies don’t want. If it’s on your list, send a message and ask.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Lake Mac Home & Roof Co. is fully licensed, qualified and insured. Licence number to add with the owner.

Which areas do you cover?

Lake Macquarie and surrounds. Recent jobs include Booragul, Charlestown, Belmont, Warners Bay, Speers Point and Toronto.

Can I send photos instead of booking a visit?

Yes, please do. Photos help us understand the job before we come out, and sometimes they’re enough to give you an idea of the price.

Do you fit gutter guard on tile roofs?

Yes. Several of the jobs on our work page show mesh gutter guard fitted along terracotta tile roofs.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text 0468 390 771, email lakemachomeroofco@gmail.com, or fill in the quote form on the contact page.

Got a job that’s been on the list too long?

Send a message with a photo or two and we’ll organise a quote. Big job or small one, it’s worth asking.
